**From:** Marcus Hale (Head of Advisory)
**Date:** Tue, Dec 16, 2025

Plea from the field: **do not bury my advisors in alerts.** Several of them absorbed big
books when our Chicago and Denver seniors left and they are underwater. If they get
40 "insights" on Monday they'll delete the email and never open it again.

Also, anything shown to an advisor has to be something they can *do* this week. "Client
X has a 23% churn probability" is useless to them. What do I say on the phone?

And please don't build something that makes advisors look bad to management. They'll
revolt.

**From:** Ellen Park (Chief Compliance Officer)
**Date:** Tue, Dec 16, 2025

A few non-negotiables from my side:

1. Anything advisor-facing is a supervised communication. I need to see what was
   generated, when, from what data, and I need to be able to reproduce it.
2. Client notes contain PII and sensitive life events. They do not leave our
   environment in raw form.
3. I'm uneasy about anything that ranks or targets clients by **age or marital
   status**. I'm not saying never, but if you use them I need to understand why and
   how we'd explain it to a regulator.
4. No language that could read as a performance promise.
